Our Service

At S Nails & Spa, we offer a full range of nail treatments designed to help every client feel confident, refreshed, and beautifully polished. Our services focus on both style and nail health, using high‑quality products and careful techniques to ensure long‑lasting, comfortable results.

Our manicure and pedicure options include classic shaping, cuticle care, exfoliation, moisturizing treatments, and a wide selection of polish colors. For clients looking for added strength or length, we provide acrylic enhancements, Gel X extensions, and builder gel overlays. Each service is performed with attention to detail, ensuring smooth finishes, clean lines, and a comfortable experience from start to finish.

We also offer custom nail art, seasonal designs, and trending styles. Whether you prefer simple elegance or bold creativity, our technicians can help you choose a look that fits your personality and occasion. Our goal is to make every appointment feel relaxing, enjoyable, and tailored to your needs.

Staff Picks

Every season, our team selects a handful of favorite styles, colors, and techniques that represent the best of what S Nails & Spa offers. These picks highlight trending looks, client favorites, and designs our technicians love creating.

This season’s top choices include soft neutral builder‑gel overlays, almond‑shaped Gel X extensions, and deep peacock blue polish — a rich, calming shade that pairs beautifully with both simple and detailed nail art. Our staff also recommends minimalist line designs and subtle chrome finishes for clients who want something modern but not too bold.

Staff Picks are updated regularly, giving clients inspiration for their next visit and helping them explore new styles with confidence. Whether you’re trying something new or returning to a favorite look, our team is always excited to help you choose a design that feels perfect for you.

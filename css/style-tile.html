Style Tile

This pagedocuments the design system used across this project: the palette, the typographic scale, and the spacing rules, so every page pulls from the same set of decisions instead of improvising fresh choices each time.

Palette

Primary Peacock Blue #0f4c64. Header background and heading color. White text on this background check at 9.7 to 1. well above the 4.5 to 1 minimum.

Neutral text #262220 on neutral background and #f8f9fa. Body text and page background. Checks at 14.5 to 1, the safest pairing in the whole palette.

Accent, Prussian #003151. Used only as a background for buttoms and small highlights, never as text setting directly on the page background, since that pairing only checks at 3.7 to 1, below the 4.5 to 1 minimum for normal text.

CTA background, a darker version of the accent, #89cfef. White text on this darker shade checks at 5.3 to 1, comfortably above the minimum instead of sitting right at the adge the way the lighter accent does.

Type Scale

Heading 1, 2.7rem

Heading 2, 2,2rem

Heading 3, 1.8rem

Body text, 2.25rem

Spacing

  • main: max-width 1000px, centered with margin: 5px auto, padding 40px top and bottom, 20px left and right.
  • Every h2: margin-bottom 1rem, keeps heading close to the paragraph it belongs to.
  • Every p: margin-bottom 1.6rem, separates one topic from the next.
  • The call-to-action button: padding 20px top and bottom, 40px left and right, plus margin-top 30px to separate it from whatever sits above it.
